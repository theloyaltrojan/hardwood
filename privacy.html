Privacy

Hardwood Legends · last updated 16 September 2026

Hardwood Legends is a browser game. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of mine holding your data — there is no backend at all. What follows is everything that leaves your browser or is kept in it.

What is stored on your device

Your progress lives in your own browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device and I cannot read it. Clearing your browser's site data for this domain deletes all of it permanently:

  • hw_career — your created player, season and career history
  • hw_bank — coins, purchases and daily-reward streak
  • hw_settings, hw_binds, hw_mode, hw_pick — options, key bindings and menu choices
  • hw_name — the display name you type for multiplayer and park chat
  • hw_seen, hw_tutdone — whether you have seen the controls card and finished the tutorial

Multiplayer and MyPark

These are peer-to-peer and opt-in: nothing connects until you open a lobby or join a code. They use PeerJS for signalling, which by default runs on PeerJS's free public server. To connect two players, that server and the people you connect to can see your randomly generated lobby code, your IP address (this is how WebRTC works — any direct connection reveals it to the other side) and the display name you chose. Chat messages go straight to the other players in your lobby. None of it is stored by me and none of it is recorded anywhere.

Advertising

This site shows ads from Google AdSense on the main menu and the post-game screen. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure those ads, and may use your prior visits to this and other sites to personalise them.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google My Ad Center.
  • More options are at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent prompt appears before any personalised ad cookie is set, and you can change your answer at any time from the link in that prompt.

Third parties the page loads

  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — serves the 3D library the game runs on, and the multiplayer library if you open multiplayer.
  • PeerJS — signalling, only if you use multiplayer or MyPark.
  • Google AdSense — ads, loaded only when an ad slot is actually shown.

Each of these sees your IP address and browser user-agent when the request is made, as is the case for any file a browser fetches.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.
